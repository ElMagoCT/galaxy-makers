# Galaxy Makers rebuild — notes for Claude

Fast static rebuild of galaxymakers.org (Durham ICC outreach exhibit). Public repo
**https://github.com/ElMagoCT/galaxy-makers**, GitHub Pages at
**https://elmagoct.github.io/galaxy-makers/** (pushes to `main` publish). Built 2026-10-15.

- The original "Simulating..." was a hard-coded 10 s `setTimeout` plus a PHP lookup
  of 27 pre-rendered EAGLE runs; `assets/js/app.js` does the lookup client-side
  (thresholds: dm <21/<54, high-mass star share <0.35/<0.70; score formula fitted
  to ±1, `tools/fit.py`).
- Pages are generated by `python3 tools/build_pages.py` (shared header/footer) —
  edit that, not the HTML. `assets/js/data.js` is generated from the original
  pages' text.
- `content/` is ~155 MB of their media (JPEG-converted); never re-encode
  `content/universe`.
- Preview: `python3 -m http.server 8797`.
